## What this tells us

1. **The site is the unit, not the sender.** One Acadia email covers 11 sites; one Pyramid email covers 3.
2. **Precision varies a lot.** Only 3 of 7 senders split by both bed type and sex. Two give one total across detox and rehab. One gives no number.
3. **0 and NA mean different things** and the board has to keep them apart: “full today” vs. “we don’t offer that.”
4. **In this sample, Allegheny County capacity is hospital-only.** Every residential bed in these emails is outside the county.
5. **Timing varies.** Reports arrived between 6:30 and 10:03 AM; three were a day old by Nov 3.
6. **Daily emails don’t describe the provider.** Location, levels of care, payers and restrictions have to come from somewhere else — the Bed Search Worksheet and provider onboarding.
